## Board 2: problem and ideas

### Problem and insight
- **AI-generated videos / images.**
  - Target: older adults / adults.
  - Problem: not being able to differentiate between AI and real videos / images.
  - Solution: a platform to make AI-generated images / videos and compare real from AI, and maybe test the differences and try to tell apart real from fake.
- "I really think that adults cannot tell the difference because they barely create with AI themselves" → learning by doing.
- The more they create with AI, the more they can identify others' AI content (like when you build a PowerPoint with Claude and you start spotting Claude PPTs).
- ★ Maybe they are able to challenge their friends to guess if what is on screen is real or AI?

### What to teach
- Game: guess is it AI or not, and why.
- Teach how to create AI content.
- Safety rules while using AI:
  - It can make mistakes
  - Not everything is true
- Teach how to run things with AI:
  - Prompts
  - Local agents(?)
  - Search engines(?)
- ★ Possibility to learn how to report such things.
- 1. Generate your own for good. 2. Detect bad. 3. Learn how to differentiate.
- AI detector to check and share scams. Generate by text stickers or something to share.
