01 · What Fenster isThree pillars: every screen belongs to one

Users learn about AI, learn by using AI (with Claude as their helper), and learn to protect themselves from AI misuse. Each pillar has its own colour and icon, so people always know which mode they are in.

📖

Learn about AI

Short lessons, the daily Real-or-fake feed, the Aha notebook.

--fs-learn · tab "Learn", "Home"

✦

Use AI, with AI

The AI helper (Claude) writes messages, answers questions, makes pictures. Always labelled.

--fs-ai · tab "Try AI"

🛡️

Protect yourself

Scam checker, safety rules, how to report. "Stop. Check. Call back."

--fs-protect · scam checker, safety lessons

Two supporting colours keep people going: Together (family, groups, sharing) and Reward (points, levels, badges).

02 · PhilosophySeven principles

We design for Anna, 72: she's clever and curious, reads with glasses, and does not want to feel stupid. When two rules clash, the higher one wins.

1

Clear before clever

One screen, one idea, one main action. Swiping is a shortcut, never the only way.

2

Calm and clean

Space, few elements, quiet colour. Only one loud thing per screen. Remove before you add.

3

Big, readable, tappable

20px text, 60px targets, a word next to every icon, bigger text one tap away.

4

Always say it's AI

Everything Claude made is violet, marked ✦ AI, and says "AI can be wrong too."

5

Never blame, always explain

"Not quite" in warm honey, never red. Every answer ends with an Aha.

6

Celebrate effort, together

Reward showing up. Cheer with family. No "last place".

7

Always a way back

"‹ Back" top-left on every screen. Nothing is lost by going back.

3 · tabsHomeLearnTry AIRankingMe
  • One column. Side by side only for equal choices (Real | Fake) or small stats.
  • Reading order = importance. Heading → what to look at → what to do. Nothing important below the main button.
  • One filled button per screen, full width, at the bottom. Others are outlined or text.
  • Same thing, same place. Back top-left, language top-right, tabs at the bottom, "Real" left and "Fake" right, explanations slide up from the bottom.
  • Spacing: 16px screen padding, 16px between blocks, 24–32px before a new heading. Group with cards, not lines.
  • Cards: 2px border, 18px corners, 16px padding, one topic. Never a card in a card.
  • At most 3 choices on a screen, 5 list items before you split.
  • Desktop: same single column, max 640px, centred. More space, not more content.
✓ DoOne heading, one picture, one big "Continue" button. Lots of space.
✗ Don'tTwo buttons of equal weight, small grey links, text over busy pictures, icons without words.

04 · ColourEvery colour has one meaning

Colour never works alone: always with an icon and a word. Contrast against white is checked live below (AA ≥ 4.5, AAA ≥ 7).

05 · TypeAtkinson Hyperlegible, big and steady

Designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision: letters like I, l and 1 never look alike. Base size 20px (23px with "Bigger text"). Line height 1.5. Bold for emphasis, never italics or all-caps sentences.

08 · ProtectCalm warnings with a clear next step

Red is reserved for real danger. Every warning says what it is, why, and exactly what to do. The habit we repeat everywhere: Stop. Check. Call back.

⚠️ Very likely a scam

A strange link and time pressure.

Do: don't click, delete it.

🤔 Be careful

We're not sure. Ask someone you trust before you reply.

✅ Looks safe

Known sender, no link, no money. Still: trust your feeling.

12 · VoiceWarm, plain, respectful

German uses "Sie". Short sentences, one idea each, no jargon. Every message says what happened and what she can do next.
